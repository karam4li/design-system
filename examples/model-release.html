Introducing DentalOS AI & Enterprise Master Patient Index

A deterministic, high-throughput identity resolution and vector retrieval architecture solving multi-practice patient fragmentation across enterprise healthcare clusters.

Inspect Evaluation Matrix ↓ View Architecture Repository

In modern multi-practice management organizations (DSOs), patient records exist in silos. Duplicated demographics, inconsistent naming conventions, and mismatched clinical IDs degrade cross-facility care coordination and break downstream billing operations.

DentalOS EMPI addresses this fundamental challenge. By fusing probabilistic record linkage (Fellegi-Sunter methodology) with 1,536-dimensional clinical dense embeddings indexed over HNSW graphs, the system resolves master records with deterministic certainty at millisecond latency budgets.

DENTALOS EMPI v2.4 4.2ms / MATCH 0.984

Incoming Payload:

{ name: "Johnathan M. Smyth", dob: "1984-11-04", phone: "555-0199" }

Result:

Resolved to canonical Master Patient Record MPI-104928. Nickname alias embedding matched "Johnathan" ↔ "John" ($cos=0.962$) combined with deterministic DOB + telephone concordances.
